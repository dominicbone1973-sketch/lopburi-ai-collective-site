Write a clear first prompt

A prompt you can adapt

Help me plan a free, informal community meetup about AI. The audience is adults with no technical background. Suggest a short welcome, two discussion questions and one simple activity. Use plain language. Mark any assumptions or details that need checking.

Then refine one thing: “Make it shorter”, “Use simpler words” or “Give me another approach”. Compare the answers and keep your own judgement.

Check before you share

  1. Find the original source, not just a link supplied by the tool.
  2. Compare important claims with a trustworthy, current reference.
  3. If something cannot be verified, say so or leave it out.

Keep personal details out

Use made-up names and sample information while learning. Don’t paste private messages or other people’s details into a tool.

Check your tool’s settings and terms before using real information. For sensitive or high-stakes questions, involve a suitably qualified person.
